FAQ

How are deposits credited?

A submitted deposit remains pending until an administrator reviews and approves it.

How are withdrawals handled?

The requested amount is reserved immediately, then the administrator moves it through review and completion statuses.

Are the five rounds configurable?

Yes. Admin can edit each of the five rounds independently, including asset, BUY/SELL action, start time, duration, profit and fee settings.

Ready to enter?

Use the client portal for account access or the separate administrator portal for platform controls.
